What is XRP?

XRP is the native asset of the XRP Ledger, a payments-focused blockchain designed for fast, low-cost cross-border transfers.

XRP trades 24/7 on centralized and decentralized exchanges. Its price is driven by supply and demand, market sentiment, liquidity, and broader crypto market conditions. Always check live data before making a transaction.

How to buy XRP (XRP)

  1. Choose a reputable exchange. Pick a platform that lists XRP and supports your country and payment method.
  2. Create and verify your account. Complete identity verification (KYC) and enable two-factor authentication.
  3. Deposit funds. Add cash or transfer crypto from another wallet, then check the fees before trading.
  4. Place your order. Use a market order for speed or a limit order to control the price you pay.
  5. Store it safely. For larger holdings, move XRP to a hardware wallet and back up your recovery phrase offline.
